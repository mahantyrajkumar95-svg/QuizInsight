About QuizInsight

Beyond Simple Scores

Traditional online quizzes simply return a final grade like 7/10 and leave students wondering which concepts they actually understood and which ones they guessed.

QuizInsight transforms passive test-taking into active diagnostic learning. By tracking topic-level accuracy, pacing, score trajectories, and knowledge retention across intervals, QuizInsight pinpoints exactly what you need to review and empowers you to study smarter.
